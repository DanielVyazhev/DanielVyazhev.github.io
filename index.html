4Under review · Information Processing & Management

Detoxification for Urdu in Nastaliq script.

Text detoxification rewrites an offensive message into a polite one while keeping what it says. Most methods and datasets are built for English. Urdu is spoken by more than 200 million people and written in Nastaliq: right to left, with letters that change shape and flow diagonally within a word. This work builds the first parallel detoxification benchmark for Nastaliq Urdu, 6.5K social-media comments with meaning-preserving rewrites, and a cascade of small models that sends only the hardest cases to a larger one.
